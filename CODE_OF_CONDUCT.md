# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and leaders of this community, are committed to
maintaining a respectful, professional, and constructive environment for
everyone.

Our community is built around shared technical interests, mutual respect,
responsible collaboration, and the values and principles upon which this project
is founded.

We welcome contributions from individuals regardless of their background,
nationality, ethnicity, age, experience, or personal circumstances, provided
that they respect the principles, rules, and values of this community.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, respectful, and
productive community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- treating all community members with respect and courtesy;
- being respectful of differing technical opinions and constructive criticism;
- giving and accepting feedback professionally;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- helping other members and sharing knowledge constructively;
- respecting the cultural, ethical, and community values established by this
  project;
- reporting bugs, vulnerabilities, and other issues responsibly;
- contributing to the project in good faith.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks;
- insults, trolling, discrimination, or deliberately disruptive behaviour;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- sharing inappropriate, explicit, or sexually suggestive content;
- promoting content or activities that conflict with the established values and
  rules of this community;
- deliberately disrupting discussions, development, or project activities;
- misusing project resources or exploiting other community members;
- any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate or harmful in a
  professional technical environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing
the standards established in this Code of Conduct.

They may take appropriate and proportionate corrective action in response to
behaviour that is considered inappropriate, disruptive, threatening, or harmful.

Community leaders reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code contributions, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
content that violates this Code of Conduct.

Moderation decisions may be accompanied by an explanation whenever appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions;
- issues and pull requests;
- community chat platforms and communication channels;
- project-related social media accounts;
- any public or private space where an individual is officially representing
  this project or community.

All members and contributors are expected to follow these rules when
participating in project-related activities.

By using this software, you acknowledge that it is experimental and that you

## Reporting Violations

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported privately to the project maintainers through the repository's
designated reporting channel.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate
confidentiality.

Community leaders will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy and
security of individuals reporting incidents.

False, malicious, or deliberately misleading reports may themselves be
considered violations of this Code of Conduct.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will determine appropriate consequences based on the severity,
frequency, and impact of each violation.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** minor inappropriate behaviour, unprofessional language, or
an isolated violation of community guidelines.

**Consequence:** a private written warning explaining the violation and
requesting corrective action.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** repeated inappropriate behaviour or a more serious
violation of community standards.

**Consequence:** an official warning. Continued violations may result in
temporary or permanent restrictions.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community impact:** serious violations, repeated misconduct, harassment, or
deliberate disruption of the community.

**Consequence:** temporary suspension from community participation and
communication for a specified period.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community impact:** severe misconduct, repeated violations despite previous
warnings, serious harassment, threats, or deliberate actions that significantly
harm the community.

**Consequence:** permanent removal from community spaces and revocation of
participation privileges.

## Project and Community Values

This project aims to maintain a community based on mutual respect, technical
excellence, responsibility, and adherence to its established principles.

Participation in the project does not grant anyone the right to disregard
community guidelines or disrupt its intended purpose.

Maintainers reserve the right to update these guidelines when necessary to
reflect changes in the project, its development, or community requirements.
